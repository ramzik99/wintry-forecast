# Wintry forecast: thorough QA and contour correction

Test date: 20 October 2026. Repository: ramzik99/wintry-forecast.

## Outcome

Testing found a real defect in the published 1000.1.9 contour generator: diagonal marching-squares saddle cells connected the wrong edge pairs. The defect is corrected in 1000.1.10 with the bilinear asymptotic determinant and deterministic exact-saddle ties. This correction applies to both map and exported-video contours.

The software checks below passed within their stated scope. This is not a claim of calibrated forecast accuracy or universal browser/device compatibility.

## Confirmed defect and regression evidence

For a unit cell with southwest/northeast values +3 and southeast/northwest values -1, the zero contour must isolate the two negative corners. Version 1000.1.9 instead isolates the positive corners. A corner-average decider also cannot generally select the correct bilinear topology in asymmetric cells.

The new tests check both sign patterns, an asymmetric cell with the opposite average/determinant topology, and exact-saddle sign invariance. On the old implementation, two tests fail and one passes; on the correction, all three pass. An additional local check on 200 deterministic saddle cells verifies elevation-offset and sign-reversal invariance.

Corrected source: a3a68ffe999f249e76a8ff4dc4d5cb41dea95dc6.

## Limits

- Browser testing used headless Chromium with software WebGL and Android touch emulation. Physical Android/iOS devices, Safari and Firefox were not tested in this pass.
- No multi-hour memory or continuous-use soak test was performed.
- Forecast calculations remain ECMWF-based even when another native Windy model is selected.
- Point-sampled snowline contours may remain angular; correcting saddle topology does not add atmospheric detail or justify smoothing across missing data.
- Software tests do not establish precipitation-type or snowfall forecast skill. Independent observations and event validation remain necessary.
